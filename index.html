              01 // KEPLERIAN ORBITS

              Undocking drops you into a circular orbit at station altitude. Your path is a conic around the current primary: PE (periapsis) is the low point, AP (apoapsis) the high point. Burning prograde at PE raises AP; burning retrograde at AP lowers PE. Burning normal tilts the plane.

              Open ORBIT NAV [N]. The green ellipse is where you are now, the cyan dashed arc is the predicted transfer, and the gold ring is the target. Pick a target orbit: the panel previews total Δv, transfer time and the burn duration at sustained thrust. Δv is pure kinematics here; the Conjoiner drive draws no propellant, so no budget blocks a burn. PLOT & AUTO-BURN flies a two-burn Hohmann transfer (or a guided continuous burn) and warps to each node if AUTO-WARP is ticked.

              The SOI switch moves the frame between the planet and the host star. Escape the planet to fly between worlds at heliocentric scale.

              02 // CONJOINER DRIVES & BRAKING ARCHITECTURE

              At constant proper acceleration a, rapidity grows linearly: η = aτ/c, β = tanh η, γ = cosh η. Ship time Δτ is far shorter than galactic time Δt.

              Braking depends on your ice shield: vessels with a single bow ice cone keep their ice prow locked forward into the interstellar hydrogen medium and slow down with reverse thrust (−g) from forward deceleration ports — they never flip 180°. Only vessels with ice on both ends (Bi-Cone / Twin-Ram) execute a 180° flip-and-burn at midpoint, pointing their aft ice shield and main aft engine bells forward to decelerate.

              The Conjoiner drive is reactionless: it taps the vacuum and throws no reaction mass, so ship mass stays constant and Δv capacity is unlimited. The operational limits are core waste heat (climbs steeply above 1.5g; cooled by fins, spin rings, and articulated radiator wings) and interstellar hydrogen (H-I) ablation. Use the Cruise β cap slider in STARMAP [M] to cap peak γ and protect your ice cone across long voyages.

              03 // HYDROGEN MEDIUM (H-I ISM), ICE & DOCKING

              Interstellar neutral hydrogen (H-I) and dust strike the ship at relativistic energies ∝ (γ−1), with ram flux rising as βγ. With unlimited vacuum-tap thrust, the cryo-ice shield is the consumable that bounds every interstellar run. Keep an ice-armored prow aimed into the hydrogen medium: bow-ice hulls brake via reverse thrust (−g), while dual-ice bi-cone hulls flip 180° onto their aft ice cone. In the Sextant [X], the H-I REF lamp overlays rest Balmer lines (H-α 656.3 nm) against Doppler-shifted target spectra.

              To repair: arrive in system, match the station orbit (STATION RENDEZVOUS in ORBIT NAV [N]), close inside the dock corridor, and press DOCK & RE-GLAZE. In DRYDOCK [Y], RE-GLAZE & COOL restores ice to 100% and cools the core. Hull refits can be previewed anywhere and commissioned while berthed for ₵ credits.
